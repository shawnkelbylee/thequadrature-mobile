The Objective: Prove the Truncation Gap. Because the average user cannot abandon the civil grid entirely, this phase isolates the extreme physiological delta between forced compliance (weekdays) and biological completion (weekends).

The Telemetry: Monitor the 21-day moving average of your HRV and RHR.

The Objective: System calibration. The algorithm finally has sufficient longitudinal data to separate your native Free Sensorium from the legacy grid's entrained fatigue.
